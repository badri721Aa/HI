[396] Checkpoints are scored on the server only. `Validator` stamps each [75] 10 Hz `ChassisRoot` sample with `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` and tests the segment between consecutive samples against the next expected checkpoint only: signed distances d0 and d1 to the gate plane must change sign and the intersection must fall inside the gate rectangle plus 4 studs of tolerance.
