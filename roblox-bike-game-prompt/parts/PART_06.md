[253] Each combustion profile holds 4 to 6 seamless loops recorded at steady idle, low, mid, high and redline RPMs, each with an on-load variant (pulling under throttle) and an off-load variant (closed-throttle engine braking at the same RPM), so 8-12 `Sound` instances per vehicle. When a vehicle model streams in, `EngineAudio` creates them once into that vehicle's `Trove` with `Looped = true` and `Volume = 0`, properties set before `Parent`, on the canonical `Exhaust1` attachment (fallback `ChassisRoot`), then in a `task.spawn` preloads them with `ContentProvider:PreloadAsync` inside `pcall` and starts them together; the local vehicle keeps all playing so crossfades never wait on playback start ([267] governs remote tiers). Loops are 1-3 s, cut at zero crossings, holding a whole number of firing cycles. Acceptance test: holding any steady RPM for 30 s yields no click, gap or phasing.

[254] Every frame `EngineAudio` turns RPM into layer gains. Layer i, sampled at r_i, plays at `PlaybackSpeed = rpm / r_i` clamped to 0.75-1.35, so it is audible only between 0.75·r_i and 1.35·r_i. The crossfade band between layers i and i+1 is [max(r_i, 0.75·r_(i+1)), min(r_(i+1), 1.35·r_i)]; with t the 0-1 position inside it, apply equal-power gains g_i = cos(t·π/2) and g_(i+1) = sin(t·π/2), so g_i² + g_(i+1)² = 1 and loudness never dips mid-sweep. Outside bands one layer plays at gain 1, holding the end layers with clamped speed. A load-time validator rejects adjacent ratios above 1.6 or bands narrower than 10% of r_i. Example, Ryujin RX-250F: layers at 1,900, 3,000, 4,800, 7,600 and 12,000 RPM; at its 13,500 limiter the top layer runs at 1.125.

[255] Engine load L blends the variants: L_target = throttle × (0.4 + 0.6·drive), where drive is clutch engagement in gear (0 in neutral, so free revs sound 40% loaded), smoothed with a 60 ms attack and 150 ms release. On-load layers receive sqrt(L) and off-load layers sqrt(1 − L), multiplied by the RPM gains of [254]. Profile volume = `Audio.BaseVolume` × (0.55 + 0.45·L) × (0.75 + 0.25·rpm/redline). Each on-load layer carries one `EqualizerSoundEffect` set from `Audio.EqPreset` (dB; Thump: LowGain +4, MidGain 0, HighGain −3; Scream: LowGain −2, MidGain +1, HighGain +3) and one `DistortionSoundEffect` with `Level` = 0.05 + 0.25·L. Exhausts ([372]) add +2 dB LowGain and +0.05 `Level` at Pro, scaled by the tier's 0.30/0.55/0.80/1.00 effect fraction. Acceptance test: rolling off at constant RPM changes timbre over 150 ms with no click or gap.
