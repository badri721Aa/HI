# Starfall Isle enemy models

Every model in `public/games/starfall/models/` is **CC0** (public domain). They were compressed with
`build_enemy_models.mjs` (glTF-Transform: unused clips removed, parts joined, quantized; 2.5 MB in all)
and are dressed in code (recolours, armour, gear, auras). Credits are also in the in-game Credits panel.
